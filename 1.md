Build a single full-viewport **React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS** page that serves as the **Role Library / Character Library (浮生卷宗 · 典籍角色库)** for the **万卷浮生 (Floating Scrolls)** project.

This screen is not a generic team page or corporate directory.

It is the sacred gallery where players discover the legendary spirits and classical masters awakened from timeless scrolls (such as *Journey to the West*, *Romance of the Three Kingdoms*, *Water Margin*, *Dream of the Red Chamber*, *Investiture of the Gods*, *Legend of the White Snake*, and *Records of the Grand Historian*), understand each persona's literary perspective and combat essence, and select their companion collaborator to step into the story and arena.

The visual direction must feel like a direct continuation of the existing **万卷浮生 welcome page**:

* immersive (沉浸东方意境)
* editorial (典雅书卷排版)
* cinematic (大片感人物立绘)
* minimal (极简克制)
* classical yet contemporary (古雅与现代审美交融)
* portrait-led (古典角色立绘/肖像主导)
* large typography (张弛有度的大字号排版)
* no dashboard feeling (无后台仪表盘感)
* no SaaS cards (无堆叠卡片)
* no excessive UI chrome (无多余冗杂界面修饰)

The transition from the welcome page into this screen should feel like stepping directly into the unfolding chronicles of the Floating Scrolls.

---

# Product intent (产品定位与核心意图)

This page is the project's **Classical Role Library (浮生卷宗 / 角色库)**.

Each portrait represents a distinct legendary persona / master from classical Eastern literature.

The interaction should communicate:

> “择一卷风骨，唤醒典籍之魂共赴浮生。”
> “Choose the legendary soul and creative perspective you want to journey with.”

Do **not** present the characters with corporate or HR semantics:

* no employee directory / staff hierarchy
* no corporate tenure / job titles
* no recruitment-style information

Instead, each character should feel like an immortal **literary collaborator & battle ally**, possessing their own philosophy, legendary prowess, and unique combat dimension.

# Animation (动效设计)

Add to CSS:

```css
@keyframes fadeIn {
  from {
    opacity: 0;
    transform: translateY(4px);
```

* No exaggerated scaling
* No distracting parallax
